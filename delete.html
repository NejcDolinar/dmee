Delete your Dmee data

Dmee: DMs Only, No Reels · by Nejc Dolinar · Last updated: 5 October 2026

How to request deletion: email app.dmee@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Dmee data". If you used invites, include your invite code (Settings → Invite friends) or your Instagram username so we can find your data. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Data you can delete yourself, right now

  • Instagram login: in Dmee, open Profile → Settings → Log out. This deletes Instagram's login cookies from your phone.
  • Progress stats, streaks and settings: stored only on your phone. Uninstall Dmee (or Android Settings → Apps → Dmee → Storage → Clear data) and they are gone.

Data deleted when you email us

  • Invite data (Google Firebase): your invite code, anonymous ID and the one-way hash of your Instagram username.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics) linked to your installation ID.

Data we keep

  • Nothing from the lists above is kept after deletion. Crash reports are also deleted automatically by Firebase after 90 days.
  • Purchases: subscription and payment records are held by Google Play, not by Dmee. Manage or cancel your subscription in the Google Play Store.
  • Your Instagram account, messages and posts belong to Instagram and are not stored by Dmee. To delete them, use Instagram's own settings.
