Dmee Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

In short: Dmee never sees your Instagram password or your messages. Your usage stats stay on your phone. We only collect anonymous crash reports so we can fix bugs (and, if you use invites, what is needed to count them). We don't show ads and we never sell data.

1. Who we are

Dmee ("the app") is made by Nejc Dolinar, Slovenia, who is responsible for your data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: app.dmee@gmail.com.

Dmee is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc.

2. Your Instagram login and messages

  • You log in on Instagram's own website (instagram.com), shown inside Dmee. Your username and password go directly to Instagram. Dmee does not read, store or send them anywhere.
  • Instagram's login cookies are stored only on your phone, like in any web browser. Logging out in Dmee deletes them.
  • Your messages, photos, contacts and profile data are loaded directly from Instagram to your phone. Dmee does not collect, store or send them to us or anyone else.
  • Dmee only changes how instagram.com looks on your phone (it hides Feed, Reels and Explore).

3. Accessibility service (optional Instagram lock)

If you turn on the Instagram lock, Dmee uses Android's Accessibility service. It is only used to:

  • detect when the Instagram app is opened, so Dmee can show its lock screen;
  • detect when you leave Instagram, so it locks again after you've posted a story or made a call;
  • detect Instagram's bottom tab bar (by its internal element ID), to return you to Dmee after posting;
  • notice that an Instagram notification arrived or the notification shade was opened (only the time, never the content), so tapping a message notification opens your DMs in Dmee.

The Accessibility service never reads text on your screen, your messages, notification content or what you type. Nothing it detects leaves your phone. You can turn it off at any time in Dmee settings or in Android settings.

4. Your progress stats

Time spent in the real Instagram app, the daily time you set during setup, streaks and the number of times Instagram was blocked are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling Dmee deletes them.

5. Crash reports

To fix bugs, Dmee uses Firebase Crashlytics by Google. When the app crashes, it sends a crash report containing technical information: the error and code location, device model, Android version, app version, and a random installation ID. It does not contain your name, Instagram account, messages or photos.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Google processes this data on our behalf and may process it outside the EU under appropriate safeguards. Crash reports are kept for 90 days. See Firebase privacy information.

6. Invite friends

If you use the invite feature, Dmee signs in to Google Firebase anonymously (no name, email or account) and stores your invite code. If you join Dmee through someone's invite, Dmee stores that code, an anonymous ID and a one-way hash of your Instagram username (the username itself can't be read back from it), so each Instagram account counts only once and your friend gets their free month. This data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and kept while the invite program runs. Legal basis: providing the feature you chose to use (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). You can ask us to delete it at any time.

7. Rule updates

To keep working when Instagram changes its website, Dmee downloads updated hiding rules from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) about once an hour. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to GitHub. No personal data is sent. See GitHub's privacy statement.

8. Photos, camera and microphone

  • Photos you pick to post are handed directly to the Instagram app. Dmee keeps a temporary copy only until it is shared, and never uploads it to us.
  • Camera and microphone access is only used by instagram.com inside Dmee, for example to send voice messages or join calls, and only when you allow it.

9. What we don't do

  • No ads and no advertising trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No analytics on what you do inside Instagram.

10. Payments

If Dmee offers a subscription, payments are handled by Google Play. We never receive your card details. We only learn whether your subscription is active.

11. Age

Dmee is intended for people aged 16 and over.

12. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. Since almost everything stays on your phone, you can delete it yourself by logging out or uninstalling Dmee. For crash reports or any question, email app.dmee@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Slovenian Information Commissioner (ip-rs.si) or your local data protection authority.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
